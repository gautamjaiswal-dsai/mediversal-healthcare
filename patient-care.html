Insurance & Cashless Information

Mediversal Healthcare works with insurance providers to support cashless treatment where applicable. Please bring your insurance ID and a valid photo ID at the time of admission; our billing desk will guide you through documentation.

Patient Rights

Every patient has the right to respectful care, clear information about their diagnosis and treatment, informed consent, privacy, and access to their medical records.

Patient Responsibilities

Patients are asked to provide accurate medical history, follow prescribed treatment plans, and communicate openly with their care team.
