About Mediversal

Advanced Healthcare. Human Care.

Mediversal Healthcare brings together clinical excellence, modern technology and compassionate care across a connected network of hospitals in Patna, Bihar.

Our Story

Mediversal Healthcare was built around a simple idea: that advanced medical care and genuine human compassion should never be separate. Across our Multi Super Speciality Hospital, Maatri maternal care centre, Health Studio and Homecare services, every unit is designed around the patient's journey — from the first consultation to long-term recovery.

Mission

To deliver accessible, transparent and clinically excellent healthcare, supported by technology and delivered with compassion.

Vision

To be Bihar's most trusted healthcare network — recognized for safety, quality, and patient-centered care.
